Holleton reaches MDCP approval milestone

VAA is advancing preparations for its proposed conventional vat-leach campaign following approval of the Mine Development and Closure Proposal.

Location
Holleton, Western Australia
Mining lease
M77/643
Proposed method
Conventional vat leaching
Proposed campaign
Up to 45,000 tonnes
Status
MDCP approved · Project preparation progressing

MDCP approval received on 16 November 2026

The Department of Mines, Petroleum and Exploration (DMPE) approved the Mine Development and Closure Proposal (MDCP) for Leary's Lament, Holleton, on 16 November 2026 under the Mining Act 1978. The proposed campaign covers conventional vat leaching of up to 45,000 tonnes of historically mined and treated gold-bearing material on mining lease M77/643.

The approval defines the campaign’s activity area, approved work and closure requirements.

Preparing for the campaign

VAA is preparing further containment and water-balance information to support the Department of Water and Environmental Regulation’s (DWER) assessment of the works approval application.

VAA is also progressing water-supply arrangements, safety systems and the authorisations needed for construction and commissioning.

FRABEN research

VAA is developing FRABEN through a separate research programme focused on producing a gold-bearing concentrate.
