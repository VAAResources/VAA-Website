About VAA Resources

Integrated capability built around gold recovery

VAA Resources Pty Ltd is a Western Australian gold reprocessing company. Our primary focus is recovering value from previously mined and historically treated gold-bearing material.

Material assessment, plant preparation, field operations, mineral processing, approvals planning and technical management are brought together to support that objective from evaluation through to operation.

Holleton survey supporting processing and site planning

How Gold Reprocessing Works

An integrated pathway from gold-bearing material to recovery

VAA brings together material assessment, controlled preparation, processing evaluation and site delivery to establish practical gold-recovery pathways for previously mined and historically treated material.

Material Assessment & Preparation

Gold-bearing material is assessed, sampled, classified and prepared to determine a suitable reprocessing pathway.

Controlled Materials Handling

Stockpiles and feed material are managed to support traceability, consistent preparation and efficient processing.

Sizing for Gold Recovery

Crushing and screening are used where required to prepare material for downstream gold-recovery processes as part of VAA's reprocessing work.

Our focus is gold reprocessing.

VAA's mobile plant supports its own gold reprocessing projects and project evaluations. VAA does not operate as an equipment-hire or general contract-crushing business.

Gold Recovery Development

Processing pathways suited to the material

VAA evaluates established and alternative mineral processing approaches according to the characteristics of the gold-bearing material. Alternative non-chemical approaches remain at an evaluation stage and support the company's broader gold recovery objective.

Holleton Gold Project

Holleton: a focused gold reprocessing project

At Holleton, VAA is advancing the assessment and reprocessing of previously mined and historically treated gold-bearing lateritic material within an established mining area.

The work is directed toward establishing a practical reprocessing pathway for Holleton's gold-bearing material. It includes material assessment, processing studies, water management, plant configuration, site infrastructure, environmental planning and regulatory approvals.

Field investigation, survey and operational planning are used to refine the project layout and practical requirements for development, with an emphasis on working within established disturbance wherever practicable.

Leadership

Peter Sworder

Chief Executive Officer & Director

Peter is a mechanical engineer, former airline captain, entrepreneur and gold-processing project leader. His Western Australian gold-processing experience dates from 1984 and includes historic tailings and laterite treatment, conventional vat-leach projects, plant selection, approvals coordination and practical project delivery.

At VAA Resources, Peter leads project development, processing strategy and operational delivery for the Holleton Gold Project. He is also evaluating alternative non-chemical mineral-processing approaches for previously treated gold-bearing material.
