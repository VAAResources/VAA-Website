About VAA Resources

Practical gold project development, from evaluation to operation

VAA Resources Pty Ltd is an Australian resources company based in Western Australia. The company is evaluating gold reprocessing opportunities focused on the recovery of value from previously mined and historically treated gold-bearing material.

VAA combines project development, field operations, mineral processing, site assessment and technical management to progress selected resource opportunities through evaluation, approvals, preparation and operation.

Holleton Gold Project

A Western Australian gold reprocessing opportunity within established disturbance

The Holleton Gold Project is focused on evaluating and reprocessing previously mined and historically treated gold-bearing lateritic material within an established mining area.

Western Australia Gold reprocessing focus Previously treated material Historically disturbed mining area M77/643 & M77/898

Project work includes mineral assessment, processing studies, water management, plant configuration, site infrastructure and environmental and regulatory planning. VAA is also evaluating alternative non-chemical mineral processing approaches that may be suited to previously treated gold-bearing material.

Field investigation, survey and operational planning are used to refine the project layout and practical requirements for development, with an emphasis on working within established disturbance wherever practicable.
