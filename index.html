About VAA Resources

Practical project development, from assessment to operation

VAA Resources Pty Ltd is an Australian resources company based in Western Australia. The company is developing projects focused on the recovery and reprocessing of previously mined mineral resources using practical, efficient and responsible operating methods.

VAA combines project development, field operations, mineral processing, site assessment and technical management to progress resource projects from evaluation through to operation.

Holleton Laterite Project

Project evaluation and development in Western Australia

VAA Resources is progressing the Holleton Laterite Project, focused on evaluating and reprocessing previously mined and historically treated lateritic material.

Project development includes mineral assessment, processing studies, water management, plant configuration, site infrastructure and environmental and regulatory planning.

Field investigation, survey and operational planning are used to progressively refine the project layout and the practical requirements for site development.

Safety & Compliance

Responsible site systems

VAA Resources is committed to responsible operations and the systematic management of workplace health and safety, dangerous goods, environmental risks and operational controls.

Site systems and procedures are developed with reference to applicable Western Australian legislation, regulatory requirements, Australian Standards and recognised industry practices.
