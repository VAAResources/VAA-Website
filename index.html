Gold reprocessing: common questions

What is gold reprocessing?

Gold reprocessing involves treating material from earlier mining or processing activities to recover remaining gold. It can involve historic stockpiles or previously treated material, such as tailings. Sampling and processing studies are needed to assess suitability; previous mining or treatment alone does not establish a viable recovery project.

What gold-bearing material does VAA consider?

VAA's focus is previously mined and historically treated gold-bearing material. At Holleton, the assessment concerns lateritic material. Each enquiry is considered on its own material characteristics, available information, site access and project requirements.

What stage is the Holleton Gold Project at?

Holleton is at the evaluation and planning stage. Work covers material assessment, processing studies, site planning and approvals requirements. Further development depends on the results of this work, commercial assessment and applicable approvals.

What should I include in a material or project enquiry?

Include the location, material type, your relationship to the project and any available sampling or processing information. A short, non-confidential overview is enough to begin a discussion. Contact details are available in corporate and project enquiries.

03 / Holleton Gold Project

A focused gold reprocessing project

At Holleton, VAA is assessing previously mined and historically treated gold-bearing lateritic material within an established mining area. The project is at the evaluation and planning stage.

Location Western Australia
Tenements M77/643 & M77/898
Material Previously mined and treated lateritic material
Project stage Evaluation and planning

The work is directed toward establishing a practical reprocessing pathway for Holleton's gold-bearing material. It includes material assessment, processing studies, water management, plant configuration, site infrastructure, environmental planning and regulatory approval requirements.

Field investigation, survey and operational planning are used to refine the project layout and practical requirements for development, with an emphasis on working within established disturbance wherever practicable.
