Gold reprocessing partnerships

Gold Tailings & Stockpile Assessment

VAA Resources is a Western Australian gold reprocessing company. Our team has extensive experience with gold-bearing material from WA’s historic mines, including tailings, stockpiles and heap-leach residues.

Through a fixed-fee desktop review, we help leaseholders and project owners interpret their existing records and decide whether further sampling or testwork is worthwhile.

Request a project review

Email corporate@vaaresources.com with a brief, non-confidential overview of the site location, material type, your relationship to the project and what records you have.

What the fixed-fee review covers

We start with one site and an agreed material area. We check whether there are enough records for a useful review. If there are, we agree the scope and fixed fee with you before work begins.

We review the available processing history, plans, photographs, assays and prior testwork. You receive a short written assessment covering:

  • What the records tell us about the material and where the evidence is incomplete.
  • Which processing options may warrant investigation.
  • Whether to pause or prioritise the next round of sampling or testwork.

Recovery and commercial viability require further investigation. We quote fieldwork, new drone surveys, sampling and laboratory testing separately if needed.
